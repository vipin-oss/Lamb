\section*{Neha: phason relaxation, bulk closed form first}

Decoupled phason, $K_1k^{2}=\rho_p\omega^{2}+\mathrm{i}\omega\gamma$, at $\omega=2\pi10^{8}$:
$c_{ph}=\sqrt{K_1/\rho_p}=31622.78$~m/s, $k_i/k_r=\gamma/(2\rho_p\omega)$ while
$\gamma\ll2\rho_p\omega=1.5\times10^{9}$, and $k_r\to\sqrt{\omega\gamma/(2K_1)}$,
$k_i/k_r\to1$ above it.

\section*{Anu: magnetoelastic coefficient against volume fraction}

The mixing rule is ours, not a cited one: the two-phase ME coefficient is taken as the product
property $\alpha(v)=v(1-v)\alpha_0$, which vanishes at both ends and peaks at $v=1/2$, and it enters
the model by scaling the three magnetoelastic constants with $f(v)=4v(1-v)$, so
$\alpha^{2}\propto f^{2}$.

Thin plate, $fd=100$, S0: 6280.67~m/s for every $v$ to 0.01~m/s. There is no measurable shift,
because at small $k$ the magnetoelastic terms reach the mechanical rows as $\eta q_{31}$,
$\eta q_{33}$ and $\mathrm{i}kq_{15}$, and $q_{15}/c_{44}$ is of order $4\times10^{-9}$.

A0 at $fd=1000$, window of $0.5$~m/s:

\midrule

$\Delta c$ (m/s) & 0 & $+0.775$ & $+1.310$ & $+0.775$ & 0\\

The shift peaks at $v=1/2$, vanishes at both ends and scales as $\alpha^{2}$ with
$\Delta c/f^{2}$ constant to five per cent; the two symmetric points agree to the last digit, so
that five per cent is the end of the quadratic regime and not scatter.

\section*{Parity and a note for the scholar file}

With all couplings on the A and S families stay separate: Neha gives A0 at 2216.6 and the S0 branch
near 5050~m/s, Anu gives A0 at 2277.1 and S0 at 6280.7~m/s, and both papers keep the single pattern
$(+,-,-,-,+)$ and its negative. Unchanged from the last round.

One sentence belongs in the scholar file when the Sumit paper is next opened: the screening density
is $n_0^{th}=\epsilon_{33}\lvert-\mathrm{i}\omega+D_nk^{2}+1/\tau_R\rvert/(qb_n)$, so it follows the
carrier lifetime, and the $1$~ns used in the papers is a placeholder until a measured lifetime
exists for the film.
