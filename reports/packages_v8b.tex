\section*{The units fault}

You were right. The three tabulated heat capacities, $2.8\times10^{6}$, $2.6\times10^{6}$ and
$2.5\times10^{6}$, are volumetric already, and the code was multiplying by $\rho$ again. The heat
capacity was five thousand six hundred times too large in Sumit, and the corresponding factors in
Neha and Anu. `rcp_of` now returns the tabulated value. Every elastic and size-effect number
reported so far was produced with the couplings off and does not move; the fully coupled runs move
a little, and they now have their own baseline, $2181.9$~m/s at $fd=1000$ against the elastic
$2326.6$.

\section*{Hutson--White: the sign was in my test}

The model is right and the masters are right. Gauss's law carries $M_{34}=q$ with the row
$\mathrm{i}kD_x+\eta D_z+q\Delta n=0$, the carrier row carries $M_{43}=b_nn_0L$ and
$M_{44}=-\mathrm{i}\omega-D_nL-\mathrm{i}kv_d+1/\tau_R$, and both match the master line for line.
The piezoelectric block is skew: $M_{23}=+e_{15}k^{2}$ and $M_{32}=-e_{15}k^{2}$ at $\eta=0$. My
hand-built test sub-block had those two entries equal, and a symmetric sub-block inverts the
physics. With the skew entry, the sub-block $(u_z,\phi,\Delta n)$ at $\eta=0$, $D_n=0$, $v_d=0$,
$f=100$~kHz, gives $k_i>0$ and a peak at $n_0=3\times10^{16}$~m$^{-3}$ against
$n_0^{*}=2.9654\times10^{16}$, with $k_i/k_r=1.73\times10^{-2}$ there against $K^{2}/2=3.6\times10^{-2}$
halved by the resonance. Sign, position and magnitude all pass, and nothing in the code or the
masters needed changing.

\section*{The closed form for the plate threshold}

Linearising the pair (Gauss, carriers) gives the screening threshold
$n_{0}^{th}=\epsilon_{33}\lvert -\mathrm{i}\omega+D_nk^{2}+1/\tau_R\rvert/(qb_n)$. Two limits follow
and both were tested. With $1/\tau_R\ll\omega$ it collapses to $\epsilon_{33}\omega/(qb_n)=n_0^{*}$,
the bulk value, which is the consistency the bulk test shows. With $1/\tau_R\gg\omega$ it becomes
$\epsilon_{33}/(qb_n\tau_R)=5.6\times10^{19}$ for $\tau_R=1$~ns, and the observed crossover between
$10^{18}$ and $10^{19}$ is its lower shoulder; the factor against $n_0^{*}$ is
$1/(\omega\tau_R)=1592$, which is the 30 to 300 we saw. Lengthening the lifetime moves it: at
$\tau_R=1$~ms and $fd=100$ the screening is complete by $n_0=10^{18}$, S0 at 5262.7 against the
short-circuit 5259.1, while at $\tau_R=1$~ns the same point still reads 5504.6.

\section*{The closure, with its own baseline}

$l_g=l_{nl}$ was the fault, as you said; with $0.5$ and $2\,l_g$ the runs are healthy. The baseline
column is the same coupled model with both lengths at zero, $2181.9$~m/s at $fd=1000$ and $943.5$ at
$fd=100$. The $l=10^{-8}$~m route to that baseline is not usable: at $l/h=2\times10^{-5}$ the
