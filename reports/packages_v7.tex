\section*{The parity result, which differs between the papers}

For Sumit the determinant is even in $\eta$ if and only if $\beta_{\rm th}=0$ and $\Pi_{\rm th}=0$. The two
entries that break the sign pattern are $M_{45}=-\beta_{\rm th}L$ and $M_{54}$, both even where the pattern
needs odd. This is a correction to item 3 of your section 2: the conjunction with a polar coupling is not
needed. The piezoelectric and pyroelectric constants respect the pairing; the thermodiffusion and Peltier
coefficients break it, each on its own. Numbers are in \texttt{internal/sumit\_matrix\_verification.md},
table of Test 3.

For Neha and Anu the determinant is even for every value of every coupling, and the sign vector is
$(-1,+1,+1,+1,-1)$ in the order (displacement along the plate, across it, the third field, the electric
potential, the temperature): the first and the last are odd through the thickness. For Neha this answers the
question in your section 6: the phonon--phason constants, the thermo-phason coefficient and the
piezoelectric and pyroelectric constants all preserve the split. For Anu the same holds for the
magnetoelectric, pyroelectric and pyromagnetic constants. Both papers therefore factorise,
$\det\mathbf B=\det\mathbf B_{\rm S}\det\mathbf B_{\rm A}$, with two 7$\times$7 blocks (5$\times$5 at
$l_g=0$).
